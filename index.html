Features

01Foundation in development

3D Modelling

Build, shape and edit hard-surface and organic models. Work on the viewport, primitives, selection and transforms has begun; full modelling tools are planned to follow.

  • Primitives, selection & transforms
  • Edit modes for vertices, edges and faces (planned)
  • Hard-surface and organic workflows (planned)
02Planned

Sculpting & Materials

Planned tools for sculpting, materials and texture painting — for turning rough forms into finished, surfaced work.

03Planned

NOVA Optimise

Planned mesh analysis and performance optimisation tools — to see where complexity lives in a model and reduce it without losing the shape you designed.

  • Mesh analysis & density insights (planned)
  • Guided polygon reduction (planned)
  • Performance checks before export (planned)
04Future

Extensions

A future ecosystem for additional tools, brushes and community-made extensions that plug straight into the studio.

Only the modelling foundation is in active development today. Everything else on this page describes planned or future functionality and may change.

Philosophy

Easy to start.
Hard to outgrow.

Most 3D software makes you choose between friendly and capable. NOVA is being built on the belief that you shouldn't have to — and that the essentials of creating in 3D should be open to everyone.

  1. 01

    Approachable for beginners

    Clear tools, sensible defaults and helpful guidance for anyone opening a 3D application for the first time.

  2. 02

    Powerful enough to grow with you

    Depth that reveals itself as your skills develop, so experienced creators have room to work without being boxed in.

  3. 03

    Core features intended to stay free

    The core creative toolset is intended to remain free — no paywall between you and the essentials of making things in 3D.
